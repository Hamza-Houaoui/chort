# CHORT v1.4 — Holy Quran with Synchronized Mosshaf & Reciters 📖✨

CHORT v1.4 introduces full integration with the **Quran.com Audio API (Quran Foundation)**, bringing the Holy Quran directly to the iPod interface with synchronized Uthmanic Mosshaf reading, whole-ayah audio tracking, and top celebrated reciters.

## What's New in v1.4

### 📖 Holy Quran (القرآن الكريم) in Home Menu
- Positioned in Home Menu:
  ```text
  1. Music
  2. Online
  3. Radio
  4. Podcasts
  5. Quran (القرآن الكريم)
  6. Listen Together
  7. Voice Memos
  8. Settings
  ```
- Instant access to all **114 Surahs** ordered 1..114 with Makkiya/Madaniya badges, verse counts, and page numbers.

### ⏱️ Synchronized Mosshaf & Ayah Highlighting (Option A & C)
- **Live Mosshaf Reading**: Complete Uthmanic script (`بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ`, Ayah end symbols `۝١`, `۝٢`, etc.).
- **Whole-Ayah Illumination**: The active Ayah lights up in warm golden illumination on an emerald green background in real time as the Sheikh recites.
- **Auto-Scrolling**: Keeps the currently recited verse centered and smooth.
- **Click-to-Seek**: Tap any Ayah or turn the Click Wheel to seek playback instantly to that specific Ayah.
- **Now Playing Lyrics Synchronization**: Switch to the Now Playing lyrics screen to follow synchronized Ayahs with live audio playback.

### 🔖 Bookmarks & Resume (العلامات المرجعية)
- Save any Ayah with the heart bookmark icon while reading.
- **"متابعة القراءة" (Continue Reading)** shortcut in the Quran menu instantly resumes your last read/listened Surah and Ayah.

### 🔍 Live Quran Search
- Search by Surah number (e.g. `18`), Surah name in Arabic or English (`الكهف`, `Kahf`).
- Search by reference (`2:255` for Ayat Al-Kursi).
- Full text search across the Holy Quran via Quran.com search API.

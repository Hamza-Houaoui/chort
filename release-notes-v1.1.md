# Podium v1.1 - Release Notes

**Podium** is a retro iPod-style music player for Android with an authentic Click Wheel experience, virtual screen shell, zero Material3 components, and zero telemetry/ads.

## What's New in v1.1

### 1. 3D Cover Flow Across All Environments
- **Offline Downloads Cover Flow**: Browse and flip through your downloaded songs with 3D covers and play offline instantly.
- **Online Cover Flow**: Explore trending and popular albums, playlists, and liked tracks in perspective view.
- **Radio Favorites Cover Flow**: Smooth 3D rack of your favorite Tunisian and worldwide radio stations with live playback.
- **Classic Music Cover Flow**: Polished physics, rotational tilt, and inertial scrolling using the Click Wheel or gestures.

### 2. Listen Together (Synchronized Listening)
- Listen to music simultaneously with friends over local Wi-Fi or LAN.
- iPod-style 4-digit tumbler session codes.
- Shared queue management where guests can suggest tracks and host broadcasts synchronized playback.

### 3. Offline Downloads & Smart Downloads
- Download individual songs, full albums, and playlists from YouTube Music for offline listening.
- Smart Downloads automatically keeps your most-played songs cached and ready offline.
- Background downloading with notifications and retry support.

### 4. Online Radio Directory (Tunisia & Worldwide)
- Curated Tunisian FM stations (Radio Tunis Nationale, Mosaique, Zitouna, KnOOz, Sabra, etc.).
- Integrated Radio Browser worldwide directory (search by name, country, and genre).
- Support for Icecast, Shoutcast (MP3/AAC/OGG), and HLS live streams with auto-redirect resolution.
- Add and edit custom stations with custom logos.

### 5. Local Music Metadata & Lyrics Editor
- Edit Song Title, Artist, and Album tags with immediate library updates.
- Edit synchronized LRC lyrics (`[mm:ss.xx]`) or plain text with one-click clipboard paste.
- Batch edit entire albums or artist names across your library.
